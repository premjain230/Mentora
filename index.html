FAQ

Questions? We've got answers.

Everything you need to know about Aquila AI before you dive in.

Is Aquila AI really free?
Yes — Aquila AI is completely free right now during Early Access. The Free plan gives you 20 AI chats and 20 analyses per day, plus 30 minutes of Study Planner time. Even Aquila Pro is available at no cost during this period — just request access and we'll upgrade you manually.
Which exams does Aquila AI support?
Aquila AI is built specifically for Indian students preparing for JEE Main, JEE Advanced, NEET, and Board exams (Class 10 & 12 — CBSE and most State Boards). Our AI tutor and quiz generator are tuned to the NCERT syllabus and official exam patterns.
How do I get Aquila Pro access?
Click "Request Pro Access" on the Subscription page or join our Discord and message the team. During Early Access, we upgrade accounts manually within 24 hours. No payment required — it's free for early supporters.
Do daily limits reset?
Yes — all Free plan limits (chats, analyses, planner time) reset every 24 hours automatically at midnight. Your usage counter starts fresh each day. Pro users have no limits at all.
How accurate is the AI tutor?
Aquila AI is powered by a large language model fine-tuned for academic content and cross-checked against NCERT material. It's very strong for conceptual explanations and problem-solving. That said, for critical facts or exam-specific rules, always verify against official NCERT books or your teacher — no AI is 100% perfect.
Is my data safe?
Absolutely. Aquila AI uses Firebase Authentication with Google OAuth, and all data is stored in Firestore with strict security rules. Your chats and study data are private to your account and never shared or sold.
Will Aquila AI become paid in the future?
The Free plan will always stay free. We plan to introduce paid Pro plans in the future, but Early Access users who join now will be given priority and generous benefits. Anyone who gets Pro access for free during Early Access keeps it for an extended period.
